Clinical reminder: In open-chain convention, pronation combines dorsiflexion, eversion and abduction; supination combines plantarflexion, inversion and adduction. Closed-chain mechanics involve additional tibial and rearfoot relationships. Actual joint axes are oblique and motion is coupled.
